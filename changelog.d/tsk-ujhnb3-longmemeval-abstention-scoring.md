### Fixed
- LongMemEval runner scored correct abstentions as wrong: the "I don't know" string filter skipped the judge for _abs questions and the judge prompt called non-answers always incorrect, capping a perfect reader at 470/500 and understating end-to-end by up to six points. Abstention questions (question_id ending "_abs") now bypass the string filter and always go to a new ABSTENTION_JUDGE_PROMPT that scores a prediction correct when it says the information is unavailable, not mentioned, or cannot be determined, and incorrect when it asserts a specific answer. Non-abstention behaviour is byte-identical to master, and the results summary now records abstention_correct and abstention_total.
