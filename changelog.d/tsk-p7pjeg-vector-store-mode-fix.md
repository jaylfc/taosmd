### Fixed
- Opening a vector store no longer rewrites it. A legacy dense store (JSON embeddings, marker without `dense_format`) is served as-is: zero rows are converted on open, the marker keeps no `dense_format` key, `add()` keeps writing JSON, and 0.4.x can still open the store. Fresh dense stores still write base64 float32 with `dense_format=f32`; stores already converted open unchanged, and mixed stores are searched row by row. The implicit float32 conversion on open (`_migrate_dense_to_f32` and its vacuum step) is removed. binary_quant and late_interaction stores write `dense_format=none` when fresh and reopen with a marker that carries `dense_format=none` or no `dense_format` key.
