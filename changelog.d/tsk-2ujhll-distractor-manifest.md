### Added
- E-048 retrieval harness now supports `distractor` manifest entries: an entry with `"distractor": true` is accepted with captions absent or empty and is embedded as a ranked item that competes with captioned items but contributes no queries. `run()` reports `n_distractors` alongside `n_items` and `n_queries`. Non-distractor entries with missing or empty captions are still refused with the original `missing key` / `empty captions list` errors; a non-bool `distractor` value raises `distractor must be a bool` and a distractor carrying captions raises `distractor entries carry no captions`.
