### Fixed

- The `prefer_verified` recall gate no longer re-sorts hits by score. Scores arrive in source-native units (BM25 source_scores and vector cosine similarities live on different scales), so the old boost-and-sort systematically demoted keyword hits below semantic hits from the unit mismatch alone: in a shadow deployment a correct rank-2 BM25 hit sank to rank 10 with the gate on (identity with it off), disappearing from top-3 recall despite its claim status being fine. The gate now does what its name implies: it drops the claim statuses each mode documents (unchanged), moves supported claim-backed hits to the front, and preserves retrieval order everywhere else. No hit's `score` is mutated in the process.
