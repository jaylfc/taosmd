### Fixed

- The regex fact extractor no longer turns agent meta-text into bogus claims (#653). A shadow deployment measured 10k regex-extracted rows of mostly noise: "Your job is to publish the REAL stories" became an `is_a` fact about an infinitive, "The full skill content is loaded below" became `is_a(...loaded below)` x886. `_clean_entity` now takes a `role`: objects whose first word is a preposition, conjunction, infinitive marker, negation, or adverb (`in|to|already|genuinely|...`) or that end on a stray particle (`below|here|today|...`) are rejected; subjects that open with a clause-initial word (`if|whether|that|when|...`) or whose head noun is vague (`job|thing|content|message|...`) are rejected. Existing quality filters and all positive extraction paths are unchanged (`tests/test_memory_extractor_junk.py`).
