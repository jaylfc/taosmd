### Added
- `taosmd.sync_merge.merge_kg`, exported as `taosmd.merge_kg`: content-keyed KG merge for account-device-sync S4 (bus 7401). Merges peer knowledge-graph entities and triples into the local store by content key (subject, normalised predicate, object), never by row id and never deleting a row. Open triples union-merge (min valid_from, max confidence, sorted source_ids union, max appeared_count); a peer closure wins over an open local row; re-asserts after closure are kept as separate intervals with fresh ids; SINGULAR_PREDICATES resolve by latest valid_from with losers stamped valid_to and superseded_by; entity type "unknown" is upgraded to the peer's typed value, never downgraded. Peer opened read-only via file:...?mode=ro; one local transaction with rollback and re-raise on error.
